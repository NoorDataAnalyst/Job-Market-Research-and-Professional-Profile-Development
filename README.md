#  Job Market Research & Professional Profile Development

A comprehensive data-driven study analyzing the global hiring landscape to map out employer expectations, tool requirements, and regional variations for data professionals.

---

##  Author
* **Noor Ul Ain Zahid** (FA24-BBD-069)


##  Project Aim & Chosen Field
The primary goal of this research is to identify the exact technical and soft skills top employers look for when hiring analytics talent This project focuses entirely on the **Marketing Data Analyst** role because it connects data science with business strategy, helping modern marketing teams turn raw numbers into useful business insights.

---

##  Methodology & Tools Used
The study analyzed 20 real job descriptions from 10 Pakistani companies and 10 foreign companies based in the USA, UAE, and Netherlands. 

The following tools were used to process the data and build a comprehensive dashboard:
* **Python:** Used to perform frequency analysis on the requirements text.
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn:** Used to design a data dashboard featuring 8 different analytical views.
* **Word Cloud Generator:** Used to create a visual keyword map of the most demanded skills.

##  Key Findings

### 1. Core Technical Skills
* Market research is the most requested technical skill, appearing in 30% of all job descriptions.
* Data analysis, predictive modeling, and statistics follow closely behind as critical technical needs.
<img width="468" height="190" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/97ef9373-2525-4e63-afa8-1c073d983872" />


### 2. Software & Platform Trends
* Excel is universally required, appearing in 55% of the postings across all markets.
* Foreign employers heavily prioritize advanced tools like SQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI.
<img width="468" height="175" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b26accb3-94b0-42b0-9054-b26b65269def" />


### 3. Regional Differences
* Pakistani companies focus more on digital marketing tasks like pay per click ads, campaign optimization, and social media analytics.
* Foreign companies place a much higher weight on statistical modeling, data engineering, and specialized platforms like BigQuery or Looker.
<img width="3270" height="1619" alt="03_regional_market_comparison" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3e33cc07-51b7-4abc-899d-eac5bdb14f92" />


### 4. Critical Soft Skills
* Both local and international companies strongly agree on human traits.
* Communication, attention to detail, and collaboration are the top three soft skills demanded globally.

##  Strategic Recommendations & Next Steps
* **Close the Technical Gap:** Aspiring analysts looking to target global markets must prioritize learning programming languages and database tools like Python and SQL.
* **Master Visualization:** Build up practical experience with BI tools like Tableau or Power BI by creating public portfolios using real-world data.
* **Balance Hard & Soft Skills:** Maintain a strong focus on core communication and teamwork abilities, as these are universally demanded by recruiters regardless of location.
